Our Roots & Mission

Celebrating Filipino Coffee Heritage

Founded with an enduring love for our archipelago’s coffee traditions, Kape Point Cafe bridges passionate highland coffee growers with mindful urban coffee lovers. We partner directly with smallholder farming cooperatives in Sagada, Atok, and Mt. Apo, ensuring ethical compensation and sustainable farming methods.

Whether you are sitting down for focused remote work, gathering with friends over brunch, or grabbing your morning ritual fuel, our cozy cafe space is designed with comfort, hospitality, and community at heart.
